QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good to know

What does a Family plan cover?

Up to five household members on one subscription. Multi-account access must be provisioned by OnlyTrusted and validated separately on iOS and Android; neither store automatically grants five OnlyTrusted accounts on purchase.

Are Android and iPhone features identical?

No. Apple and Android expose different protections. SMS inbox scanning is Android-specific (iOS blocks third-party apps from reading SMS); iOS uses approved filtering and sharing extensions where implemented. Features such as app shielding, Wangiri auto-block, and tracker detection have platform-specific limits documented inside the app.

Can I manage or cancel my subscription?

Yes. Use the store's subscription management screen for the account that purchased the plan. Cancellation and renewal rules are displayed by the store at purchase time.

Do yearly plans renew automatically?

Yes — all paid tiers are auto-renewing subscriptions. The native checkout sheet shows the exact period, terms, price, and cancellation instructions before you confirm.

Does OnlyTrusted ever send my data anywhere?

No. The app has no network capability at all for its core features — no account creation, no cloud sync, no analytics, no crash reporter that transmits data. In-app purchases flow through Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit (your store account, not us); carrier SMS and call screening use your own carrier channel; nothing is routed through a VanPaulTek server because there isn't one.
