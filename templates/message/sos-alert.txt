OnlyTrusted — SOS alert.
Sent to all Trusted Contacts when the SOS button is pressed and the 3-second countdown completes.

Placeholders: {{owner_name}} {{timestamp}} {{approx_location_url}}

----- MESSAGE BODY (max 320 chars so it fits two SMS segments) -----

⚠️ OnlyTrusted SOS from {{owner_name}}: triggered at {{timestamp}}. Approx. location: {{approx_location_url}}. If you can't reach them, call 911. Guide: onlytrusted.app/guide#sos

----- NOTES -----

- Carriers may split long SMS into multiple segments. The body is intentionally under 320 chars (2 segments at 160 each on GSM-7, or 1 segment on UCS-2 if emoji present pushes to UCS-2).
- The ⚠️ emoji forces UCS-2 encoding — 1 segment is 70 chars. Keep the body short.
- Location is a short URL (goo.gl / app-signed URL) rather than coordinates for readability.
- The "onlytrusted.app/guide#sos" link lets the recipient read what SOS means + what to do, even if they've never heard of OnlyTrusted.
- Rate / review links are intentionally omitted — this is a safety message, not a marketing moment.
