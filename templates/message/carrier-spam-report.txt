OnlyTrusted — carrier spam report.
Sent via the Call Directory Extension's "Report as spam" flow to the carrier's spam-report shortcode (e.g. US 7726 / UK 7726 / IN 1909). The user picks "Report to carrier" from the Blocked tab's call row.

Placeholders: {{spam_phone_number}} {{report_timestamp}}

----- MESSAGE BODY (short — carriers truncate at ~140 chars) -----

{{spam_phone_number}}

----- NOTES -----

- Most carriers expect just the phone number in the body — they match the reporter's number against known-spam databases.
- Some carriers accept "SPAM <number>" or "JUNK <number>" — the Blocked tab's Call row sends whichever format the user's carrier is configured for.
- OnlyTrusted does NOT send the user's identity or the full call log — only the 10- or 11-digit number being reported.
- The carrier handles the message silently; the user does not receive a reply SMS.
- Guide: onlytrusted.app/guide#carrier-spam-report (shown in the Blocked tab UI, not in the sent SMS).
